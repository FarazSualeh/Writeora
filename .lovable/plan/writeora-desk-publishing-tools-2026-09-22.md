# Writeora Desk publishing tools

## What will be built
- Turn the Desk into a publishing workspace for Admins and invited Authors.
- Add an article library showing drafts and published stories, with clear status, dates, edit, view, and delete actions.
- Add a mobile-friendly article editor for headline, slug, excerpt, body, cover image, category, tags, reading time, and all SEO fields.
- Add Save draft and Publish actions; publishing sets the date and makes the article available on public pages immediately.
- Keep contributor invitations and access controls in a separate Admin-only area.

## Access behavior
- Admins can manage every article and contributor.
- Authors can create and manage only their own articles, enforced by the existing account permissions.
- Readers cannot open the Desk and continue to use Last read instead.

## Mobile behavior
- Stack editor fields and action controls on small screens.
- Keep the headline and primary actions visible without horizontal clipping.
- Convert article rows into compact mobile layouts while retaining all actions.

## Validation
- Verify draft creation, editing, publishing, and deletion with a signed-in account.
- Check the Desk and editor at desktop and mobile viewport sizes.
- Confirm the public article page displays a newly published story.
